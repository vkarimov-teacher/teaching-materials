part a · networking

How data travels

A network is just devices sharing data over links. To keep the complexity manageable, we split communication into layers — each does one job and hands off to the next. The reference map is the OSI model (7 layers); the model the internet actually runs on is TCP/IP (4 layers).

part b · security

Keeping it safe

Now the second half. "Secure" isn't one thing — it's a set of guarantees about information. The classic three are the CIA triad. Click each to see how it breaks and how we protect it.

Real systems need more than three. The triad is extended with:

Authentication — proving who you are Authorization — what you may do Accounting — logging what was done Non-repudiation — you can't deny an action Authenticity — the source is genuine
